Upstream source: Workout Guide traced some of its first frames from the Everkinetic
exercise drawings (https://github.com/everkinetic/data), which are also under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Workout Guide's ATTRIBUTION.md names this source, and its manifest.json records the
source and the changes for each traced frame:
https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide/blob/aac599224bb9780305239607ef98540b7e0ce389/packages/workout-guide/manifest.json
Four of the files here are among them. For each one, Workout Guide rasterised the
Everkinetic drawing on a transparent 512 x 512 canvas, recoloured it for monochrome
display and vector-traced it:

  glute-bridge-1.svg          traced from Everkinetic 0109-tension.svg
  leg-curl-1.svg              traced from Everkinetic 0117-tension.svg
  machine-chest-press-1.svg   traced from Everkinetic 0066-tension.svg
  seated-row-1.svg            traced from Everkinetic 0025-tension.svg

Workout Guide credits the other files to Bryl Lim.

Changes: none. Each file is a byte-for-byte copy of the original at the commit above.
Only the names differ: assets/<exercise>/frame-<n>.svg is saved here as <exercise>-<n>.svg.
The drawings are white on a transparent background. At display time Steady uses a CSS
filter to show them in black in light mode and dim them to 85% brightness in dark mode,
which leaves the files themselves unchanged.
